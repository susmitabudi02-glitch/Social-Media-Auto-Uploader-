Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Social Media Auto Uploader ("the App") is a tool that helps its owner upload and schedule videos to social media accounts such as TikTok, YouTube and Facebook. This policy explains what information the App uses and how it is handled.

Information we collect

When you connect a social media account, the App receives authorization tokens and basic account details (such as your account name and ID) from that platform. The App also handles the video files, titles and descriptions you choose to upload.

How we use information

Information is used only to log in to your connected accounts and to publish the videos you select. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties. Data is sent only to the social media platform you choose to publish to (for example TikTok, YouTube or Facebook), and is subject to that platform's own privacy policy.

Data storage and security

Authorization tokens are stored securely and used only by the App. Videos are processed only to complete the upload and are not kept longer than needed.

Your choices

You can disconnect the App at any time by removing its access in your TikTok, YouTube or Facebook account settings. You can also ask us to delete any data we hold about you by contacting us below.

Children

The App is not intended for children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page.
