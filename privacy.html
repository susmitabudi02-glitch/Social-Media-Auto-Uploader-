Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Social Media Auto Uploaders respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information when you connect and use your own social media accounts through authorized APIs.

Information We Access

When you authorize a supported social media platform, the application may receive basic account information and authorization credentials required to provide the requested functionality.

How We Use Information

Information received through authorized platform connections is used only to authenticate your account, identify your connected account, manage authorized publishing activities, and provide the application's requested functionality.

Your Content

The application is designed to help you manage and publish content that you choose to provide. You remain responsible for the content you upload or publish.

Third-Party Platforms

Social Media Auto Uploaders uses official APIs provided by third-party social media platforms. Those platforms have their own privacy policies and terms of service. Their handling of information is governed by their respective policies.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information accessed through authorized platform connections is not used for purposes unrelated to the application's functionality.

Account Disconnection

You can disconnect or revoke the application's authorization through the applicable social media platform. After authorization is revoked, the application will no longer be able to use that authorization.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the application. However, no electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality or legal requirements change. Updated versions will be published on this page.
