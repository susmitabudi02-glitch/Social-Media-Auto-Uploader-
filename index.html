Social Media Auto Uploaders

Social Media Auto Uploaders is a desktop content management tool designed to help users manage and publish their own photos and videos through authorized social media platform APIs.

Authorized Access

Users connect their own social media accounts through official authorization systems provided by each platform.

Photo and Video Publishing

The application helps users manage and publish their own content through authorized platform APIs.

User Control

Users remain in control of their connected accounts and can revoke authorization through the applicable platform.
